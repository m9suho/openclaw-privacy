# OpenClaw

OpenClaw is a private, single-user personal automation tool. It connects to the owner's own Google account (Calendar, Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Contacts) from the owner's own computer to automate personal and work routines such as schedule reminders and document lookups.

- It is not a public service and is used by one person only: its owner.
- It runs locally on the owner's device. No data is sent to the developer or any third party.
